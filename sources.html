How a source gets approved

A new outlet is added to Pending Review when it's cited for the first time. From there it's checked against three criteria: no hard paywall, an established editorial track record, and freedom from a pattern of retracted or unreliable reporting. Sources that pass move to the approved list above; sources that fail are marked excluded with a reason. This list is the single source of truth both for manual review and, eventually, for automated curation — nothing gets linked on AInetWatch from a domain that isn't approved here.
