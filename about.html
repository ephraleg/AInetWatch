About AInetWatch

AInetWatch is a one-page news aggregator dedicated entirely to artificial intelligence: model releases, frontier labs, chips and infrastructure, policy and regulation, funding, and security. It follows the format pioneered by the Drudge Report — a dense wire of hand-picked headlines that link straight out to the original reporting, with no accounts, no feeds to manage, and no algorithm deciding what you see.

How it works

Every headline on the front page is sourced from a named outlet and links directly to that outlet's original article. AInetWatch does not republish, rewrite, or claim authorship of any linked story — it only aggregates and links, the same way Drudge does. The top banner headline is reserved for the single biggest AI story of the moment; the three columns below sort the rest into Labs & Models, Chips & Infrastructure, and Policy & Security.

Updates

The page is refreshed as news breaks. Older headlines roll off the front page and into the Archive, organized by date.

Tips & corrections

Spot a broken link, a dead story, or have a tip on a story we should be watching? Email info@ainetwatch.com.
